      Game Log

      V1.7

      V1.8: Ranked & the Hub ★

      • ★ Ranked: Bronze I, II, III, then Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond, all the way up to Champion. Win to gain RP, place low to lose some. Log in to play.
      • 🏟️ The Hub: the main menu is now a place you can walk around! Walk to a pad and press E (or tap) to play Ranked, Practice, open the Battle Pass and more. Visit the Armory at the end of the hall to change your loadout.
      • 🏆 New Ranked leaderboard in Settings.
      • 👥 Shared hub: everyone on the main menu is in the same hub. Walk around together, and press Enter to chat!

      V1.7: The Realism Update 🔫

      • 🔫 Realistic guns: round barrels, scopes, tubes and muzzles.
      • 🏟️ 3D Lobby: your character stands on a floating stage holding your gun. Drag to spin them. In an online room, everyone stands on the stage together!
      • 🗺️ Harvest Valley is now 600 x 600 (4 times bigger again) with more towns, farms and loot, and the storm moves faster.
      • 🚌 Fixed: players now spawn on the Drop Bus online (including mobile).
      • 🦃 No more build buttons in Feast Defense on mobile.
      • 🤖 Bots no longer vote in Practice. Your pick wins.

      V1.6: Feast & Freefall 🦃🪂

      • 🔐 Accounts: sign up to save your crystals, guns, skins, stats, Battle Pass, friends and maps forever, and play on any device. Your current progress comes with you.
      • 🏆 The server leaderboard is now saved forever for players with accounts.
      • 🗺️ Harvest Valley: a giant new Battle Royale map, about 14 times bigger, with towns, farms, forests and lookout towers.
      • 🚌 Drop Bus: every Battle Royale starts on a bus floating under a hot-air balloon. Press Space to jump, skydive, and your glider opens near the ground.
      • ❤️ Everyone has 500 HP in Battle Royale. Health packs heal 100 and the storm hits harder to match.
      • 🦃 Feast Defense replaces Zombies: team up to protect the Thanksgiving feast from wild turkeys, Tom turkeys and the boss GOBBLEZILLA, who throws pies from the sky.

      V1.5: The Harvest Update 🦃

      • 🪂 Battle Royale: one life, loot weapons and health packs, and outrun the shrinking storm. Last one standing wins!
      • 🏅 Weapon Mastery: rank up every gun from Bronze to Diamond. Gold and Diamond unlock special camos.
      • 👥 Friends: add friends with your friend code, see who's online, join their lobby and send invites.
      • 🛠️ Map Editor: build your own maps, test them against bots, and play them with friends in private rooms.
      • 🌾 New fall map: Harvest Farm, with red barns, hay bales, a corn field and falling leaves.
      • 🦃 Turkey Launcher (limited, until Dec 1): launch a turkey bodyguard that follows you for 8 seconds, pecks nearby enemies, and makes you take 25% less damage.
      • 🎮 3 new modes: Gun Game, King of the Hill and Capture the Flag.
      • 🎟️ Battle Pass (Season 1: Harvest): 30 tiers of rewards. Earn XP every match. Premium track unlocks for 1,000 💎.
      • 💃 Emotes: press B (or EMOTE on mobile) to Wave, Dance, Laugh or Flex.
      • 🎥 Killcam: see who got you and how much health they had left.
      • 📡 Shot indicators: arrows show where nearby gunfire is coming from.
      • 🪜 Builds now collapse: break the bottom ramp and everything above falls.
      • 💥 Land mines: press M (or MINE on mobile) to place one. 2 per life, they arm after 1 second and blow up when an enemy or zombie steps close.
      • 🪂 Fall damage from big drops. 💚 Green ammo crates now also give +5 HP.
      • 🧟 Zombies rebalanced: hard but beatable. Smaller waves, slower zombies, full health at the start of every wave, and a Giant (2,000 HP, more with bigger teams) with fewer, slower bombs.
      • 🍁 Fall skins: Maple Leaf, Harvest Plaid, Pumpkin Spice, plus the Golden Gobbler Mythical. New hats and the Fall leaves kill effect.
      • 🔭 Mobile: hold FIRE to scope with the sniper, aim, and release to shoot.
      • ✦ Mythical skins now pulse and sparkle.

      V1.4

      V1.4: Rewards Revamp

      • 🏆 Top 3 bonuses: 100 / 50 / 25 Clash Crystals for 1st, 2nd and 3rd (matches with 4+ players).
      • 🤝 Assists are here! Help with a kill to earn 1 crystal.
      • 💎 Kills give 2 crystals and headshot kills give 3.
      • 📋 Daily Missions like Zombie Slayer, Sharpshooter and Grim Reaper.
      • 🧟 Every zombie wave now has double the zombies.
      • ⚔️ The Scythe is reworked: right-click to dash, faster movement and a brand new look.
      • ✦ Mythical skins for every weapon: Dragonfire, Voltage, Starfall, Crimson Viper, Headless Horseman and more (16 total).
      • ✨ Revamped menus, kill feed weapon icons, results podium and K/A/D scoreboards.

      V1.3

      V1.3: The biggest update ever!

      • 🎃 Zombies mode on the new Halloween map, Pumpkin Hollow. Survive 5 waves and the Giant.
      • 🗺️ 2 new maps: Temple Ruins and Crystal Caverns.
      • 🔫 3 new weapons: Halloween RPG (limited), Scythe and Portal Gun.
      • 💎 Clash Crystals now appear on maps! Grab them for bonus crystals.
      • 🌈 New Rainbow skin. Skins and guns cost more.
      • 🛒 New Shop with damage, fire rate and reload stats.
      • 💣 Grenades buffed (bigger blast, 3 per life). Minigun is smaller and a bit slower.
      • ❤️ Health bar moved to the bottom left during play.
