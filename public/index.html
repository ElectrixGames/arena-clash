Game Log V1.4

V1.4: Rewards Revamp

  • 🏆 Top 3 bonuses: 100 / 50 / 25 Clash Crystals for 1st, 2nd and 3rd (matches with 4+ players).
  • 🤝 Assists are here! Help with a kill to earn 1 crystal.
  • 💎 Kills give 2 crystals and headshot kills give 3.
  • 📋 Daily Missions like Zombie Slayer, Sharpshooter and Grim Reaper.
  • 🧟 Every zombie wave now has double the zombies.
  • ⚔️ The Scythe is reworked: right-click to dash, faster movement and a brand new look.
  • ✦ Mythical skins for every weapon: Dragonfire, Voltage, Starfall, Crimson Viper, Headless Horseman and more (16 total).
  • ✨ Revamped menus, kill feed weapon icons, results podium and K/A/D scoreboards.
V1.5 coming soon!

V1.3

V1.3: The biggest update ever!

  • 🎃 Zombies mode on the new Halloween map, Pumpkin Hollow. Survive 5 waves and the Giant.
  • 🗺️ 2 new maps: Temple Ruins and Crystal Caverns.
  • 🔫 3 new weapons: Halloween RPG (limited), Scythe and Portal Gun.
  • 💎 Clash Crystals now appear on maps! Grab them for bonus crystals.
  • 🌈 New Rainbow skin. Skins and guns cost more.
  • 🛒 New Shop with damage, fire rate and reload stats.
  • 💣 Grenades buffed (bigger blast, 3 per life). Minigun is smaller and a bit slower.
  • ❤️ Health bar moved to the bottom left during play.
